★ Project Aces OST

17 Jul, 2026 • 96 words • 1 min

Project Aces (from the Ace Combat series) has one of the best videogame OSTs ever.

I am1 finally pleased2 to find their whole collection available on Spotify as of 2026. Previously, only a handful of them were available.

No more fiddling random playlists on YouTube Music!

See also: radio. ∎

  1. I’m not the only one. ↩︎

  2. I am also very pleased about the upcoming release of Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve (Oct 2nd, 2026). I wasn’t aware of it until now! Too bad I don’t currently have any hardware to play it, and console prices keep rising. ↩︎
