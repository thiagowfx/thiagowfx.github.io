What is taking much space on your Linux/BSD system?

13 Sep, 2014 • 75 words • 1 min • updated 03 Mar, 2026

⚠️ This post is over one year old. It may no longer be up to date or relevant. Opinions may have changed.

This program is so nice that I had to create a blog post just to tell people about it: ncdu. It is the most awesome and easiest (really easy! I would indicate it to any new Unix user as well) program to detect which folders/files are eating too much disk space on your system. Available for dozens of platforms. TL;DR and tutorial: just install, then type ncdu and wait for the best (worse?). See screenshots. ∎
