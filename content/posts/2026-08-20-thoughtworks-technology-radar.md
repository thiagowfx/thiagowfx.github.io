**TIL**: The [ThoughtWorks Technology
Radar](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-us/radar) publishes an yearly[^1] opinionated
report about the technology landscape today.

[^1]: Actually, biyearly (=twice a year).

It is a rich source of inspiration to identify techniques, tools, platforms
and languages & frameworks to assess and/or adopt.

They also publish PDFs and keep [historical
editions](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-us/radar/archive) available.

Too bad there isn't a RSS feed though, not that I could find.

That's what we have [Kill the Newsletter](https://kill-the-newsletter.com/) for,
right?
