Today I super serendipitously stumbled upon
[spacemacs](https://github.com/syl20bnr/spacemacs). I just cloned and tried it
for a few seconds, and I think I already love it.

I tried dozens of emacs distributions, plug-ins and workflows, but this one
looks much more different than anything I ever tried before. I am looking
forward to use it and see if it is really good or not.

**Edit (2015-04-19):** I'm not using it anymore. My passion for it just lasted
for a couple of days. However, I super liked its idea, comparing it to other
    "emacs distributions".
